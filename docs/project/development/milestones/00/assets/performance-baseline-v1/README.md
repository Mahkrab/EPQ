# Laptop campaign retained as secondary evidence

This is the original Intel Core 7 150U laptop collection, completed on 15 October 2026 from
10:01:57 to 10:15:17 UTC. Its raw data, dataset, correctness result and generated report are unchanged.
The Phase 7 completion checkpoint retains this campaign as it existed at the time.

The [PC campaign](/docs/project/development/milestones/00/assets/performance-baseline-pc-v1/README.md)
is now the primary performance baseline. Use its report and samples for the project's current
measurement summaries and subsequent same-device backend comparisons. Do not combine the two
machines' samples or present their difference as an algorithmic speedup.

The laptop completed the full sampling policy through 4,096 particles. Its two 8,000-particle attempts
stopped at the invocation budget and remain useful evidence of this device's bounded run envelope;
they do not describe the PC, where the full 8,000-particle workload completed.
