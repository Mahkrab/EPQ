# S081 — Rudman (1998)

## Full reference:

Rudman, M. (1998) *A volume-tracking method for incompressible multifluid flows with large
density variations*. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Fluids, 28(2), pp. 357–378.
DOI: 10.1002/(SICI)1097-0363(19980815)28:2<357::AID-FLD750>3.0.CO;2-D.

#### https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/%28SICI%291097-0363%2819980815%2928%3A2%3C357%3A%3AAID-FLD750%3E3.0.CO%3B2-D

## Relevance:

An established fine material-grid alternative for a future coherent staggered-volume design.
It was considered after the local averaged-mass coupling failed; it was not implemented here.

## Reliability:

Primary peer-reviewed research. Only the publisher abstract and bibliographic record were read,
on 29 October 2026; the full paper was not obtained. The abstract supports the stated design
direction, not detailed equations, local physical qualification or RTX 4070 performance.

## Tags

`vof`, `staggered-grid`, `mass-conservation`, `multifluid`

## Reading notes

- Describes a material reconstruction grid twice as fine as the velocity/pressure grid, with
  momentum transport and a pressure-correction solve.
- A future implementation must independently demonstrate geometry, conservation and convergence;
  citing this alternative is not evidence that Maelstrom has implemented it.
