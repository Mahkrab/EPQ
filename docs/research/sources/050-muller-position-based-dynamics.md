# S050 — Müller et al. (2006)

## Full reference:

Müller, M., Heidelberger, B., Hennix, M. and Ratcliff, J. (2006) ‘Position Based Dynamics’, in Mendoza, C. and Navazo, I. (eds.) *Proceedings of the Third Workshop on Virtual Reality Interactions and Physical Simulations (VRIPHYS 2006)*, pp. 71–80. Eurographics Association.

#### https://doi.org/10.2312/PE/vriphys/vriphys06/071-080

## Relevance:

Provides the general position-based simulation algorithm beneath PBF, including inverse-mass-weighted constraint projection, predicted positions, collision constraints, iterative solving and velocity reconstruction.

## Reliability:

Primary peer-reviewed workshop paper published by the Eurographics Association. It introduced the position-based dynamics method later specialised for fluids by Macklin and Müller.

## Tags

`position-based-dynamics`, `constraint-projection`, `solver`, `collisions`

## Reading notes

- Defines prediction, inverse-mass-weighted constraint projection, iterative constraint solving and velocity
  reconstruction from corrected positions.
- Supports projecting penetrated points back to valid locations; Maelstrom's ordered unit-plane operator and
  transactional failure policy are project-specific specialisations.
