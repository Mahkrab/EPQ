# S075 — Burr, Akylas and Mei (n.d.)

## Full reference:

Burr, K.P., Akylas, T.R. and Mei, C.C. (n.d.) *Rayleigh problem — diffusion of vorticity due to
no slip at boundary*. Chapter one, Modules on High Reynolds Number Flows, MIT I-campus
School-wide Program on Fluid Mechanics. Six-page institutional teaching PDF.

#### https://web.mit.edu/fluids-modules/www/highspeed_flows/ver2/bl_Chap1.pdf

## Relevance:

Provides the Navier–Stokes reduction to viscous diffusion when streamwise gradients and
transverse velocity vanish. Establishes a physical reference independent of PBF projections.

## Reliability:

Primary institutional teaching material by the named authors. Read pages 1–3, particularly
equations 1.1–1.11, on 29 October 2026. The notes solve an impulsively moving plate, not the
project's finite channel. The project's exponential sine/cosine eigenmodes follow by direct
substitution into the same diffusion equation with explicitly specified channel conditions.

## Reading notes

- No-slip prescribes tangential fluid velocity at the solid surface.
- For the stated unidirectional flow, nonlinear convection vanishes exactly.
- Equation 1.9 is du/dt = nu d²u/dy². The experiment independently checks exponential decay
  and integrated traction for each finite-channel eigenmode.
- A diffusion timescale depends on viscosity and length; these notes do not justify a universal
  five-second settling deadline or a deadline for nonlinear colliding fluid blocks.
